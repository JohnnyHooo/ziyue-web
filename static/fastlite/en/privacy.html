1. General Provisions and Scope

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how the FastLite iOS application (including its home screen widgets and Apple Watch app, collectively the "App") handles information relating to you. The App is developed and published by YongFang Xu ("we," "us," or "our").

This Policy applies only to the App itself. It does not apply to any service provided by Apple (including the App Store, iCloud, the Health app, and notification scheduling), nor to any other app installed on your device. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Please read this Policy in full before using the App. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, please stop using the App and delete it.

2. Definitions

  • Personal information: any information recorded electronically that, alone or in combination with other information, can identify a specific natural person or reflect the activities of a specific natural person.
  • Collect: in this Policy, refers specifically to information leaving your device and being transmitted to a location that we or a third party can access. Data that is stored only on your own device and that we have no means of accessing is not "collected" within the meaning of this Policy.
  • Local to your device: refers to the App's sandbox container on your iOS device, including the App's private directory and the App Group container shared among the App's components.

3. We Do Not Collect Any Personal Information

The App has no servers, no account system, no sign-up or login, and no cloud sync. We do not operate any backend service and are therefore technically incapable of receiving, storing, or reading your data. Everything you create in the App is written only to your own device.

Specifically, we do not collect any of the following:

  • Identity information such as your name, gender, date of birth, government ID number, phone number, email address, or home address;
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, UDID), device model, operating system version, carrier, or IP address;
  • Geolocation (the App does not request any location permission);
  • Contacts, photo library, camera, microphone, calendar, or Bluetooth (the App does not request any of these permissions);
  • Your fasting, weight, and water intake records, or any other health-related data;
  • App usage behavior, screen navigation paths, tap heatmaps, or retention and conversion data;
  • Crash logs and performance data (we have not integrated any crash reporting service).
Note: If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" under iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide us with aggregated and anonymized crash statistics and download figures. This data is gathered and anonymized by Apple, contains no personally identifiable information, and cannot be used by us to identify any individual user. You can turn this off at any time in that system setting.

5. Data Sharing Between App Components (App Group)

The App's main program, home screen widgets, and Apple Watch app need to display the same state (for example, how much time remains in the current fast). To make this possible, the data described above is stored in a local container shared among these components (which Apple calls an App Group).

This container remains entirely on your device and is protected by the iOS sandbox. Only the App's own components can access it; no other app, and not we ourselves, can read it. State synchronization between your Apple Watch and iPhone takes place directly over Apple's device-to-device channel (WatchConnectivity) and does not pass through any server.

6. Health Data (HealthKit)

The App can exchange data with the built-in iOS Health app. This feature depends entirely on your explicit authorization, and reading and writing are not enabled automatically by default. On the system authorization sheet, you can allow or deny each data type individually.

6.1 Data We Read

  • Weight: used to pre-fill your current weight during onboarding and to add weight entries recorded from other sources into your trend chart.
  • Water intake: used to sync water intake you have logged via Apple Watch or other apps, so you do not have to log it twice.

6.2 Data We Write

  • The weight and water intake entries you log yourself in the App.

6.3 Commitment to Limited Use

In accordance with Apple's applicable requirements and as our commitment to you, data read from the Health app:

  • is used only to present health and fitness features to you personally within the App;
  • is never used for advertising, marketing, user profiling, or similar purposes;
  • is never sold, rented, or disclosed in any form to third parties, including data brokers, insurers, and employers;
  • never leaves your device.

6.4 Revoking Authorization

You can turn off any read or write permission at any time under iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › FastLite, or turn off the master switch on the App's "Sync to Health" screen. Once revoked, the App will immediately stop the corresponding reading and writing.

Please note: revoking authorization does not delete historical data that has already been written to the Health app. If you wish to delete it, please do so yourself in the Health app. We have neither the right nor the ability to delete it on your behalf.

7. Notifications

All reminders in the App are iOS local notifications, scheduled and triggered locally by your device at the times you set.

  • The App does not use the Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and neither generates nor uploads a push token;
  • No server of any kind sends messages to your device;
  • Notification content is generated locally and neither contains nor transmits any personal information.

You can turn off reminders individually on the App's "Reminders" screen, or revoke all notification permissions for the App in iOS Settings.

8. Purchases and Subscriptions

The App's Membership features are provided through in-app purchases on the Apple App Store, and every transaction is processed entirely by Apple.

  • We do not handle, receive, or store your payment method, card number, billing address, or Apple Account information;
  • The App uses only the interface provided by Apple to read a single result, whether a valid purchase is currently held, in order to unlock features locally;
  • This result is stored locally on your device solely as a status flag.

The processing of purchase-related information is governed by Apple's privacy policy and terms.

9. No Third-Party SDKs or Tracking Technologies

The App does not integrate any third-party software development kit (SDK), including but not limited to: analytics (such as Firebase, Umeng, or Amplitude), advertising and attribution (such as AppsFlyer or Adjust), crash monitoring (such as Sentry or Bugly), social login, customer support systems, or content push services.

The App does not track your activity across other apps or websites and does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), and therefore will not present you with an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization request.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App is a native iOS application. It contains no content pages presented in web views and does not use cookies, web beacons, local storage tracking, or any other web tracking technology.

11. iCloud and System Backups

If you have enabled iCloud Backup for your iPhone in system settings, or you back up locally to a computer, that backup may include the data the App stores on your device.

This backup process is controlled by iOS and by you, and the data is encrypted and held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We take no part in this process and cannot access any of your backups. You can manage what is backed up under iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud.

12. Data Security

The App relies on the security mechanisms provided by iOS to protect your data: app sandbox isolation, system-level file protection, and your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

A risk you should be aware of: because the data is stored locally and the App does not have its own separate passcode, anyone who can unlock your device can view all of the records in the App. Please safeguard your device and your unlock credentials. In addition, jailbreaking a device compromises the iOS sandbox protections, and we cannot guarantee data security on such devices.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is no server-side retention period to speak of. Retaining and erasing your data is entirely in your hands:

  • Delete individual entries: delete a single fasting, weight, or water intake record on the corresponding screen in the App;
  • Erase everything: delete the App from your device, and iOS will remove all data in its sandbox container and App Group container along with it;
  • Health data: data already written to the Health app should be managed within that app.

Deletion cannot be undone. The App keeps no copy in the cloud; once data is deleted, it is permanently lost, and we cannot recover it for you.

14. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

We wish to emphasize that intermittent fasting is generally not appropriate for minors who are still growing and developing. We do not recommend that minors use the App to fast. If you are the parent or guardian of a minor and discover that they are using the App, please help them stop using it and delete the App.

15. Your Data Rights

In most jurisdictions (including under China's Personal Information Protection Law, the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), among others), you have rights over your personal information, including the rights to be informed, to access, to rectify, to erase, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent.

With respect to the App, because we do not collect, receive, or store any of your personal information, there is nothing held by us against which these rights could be exercised. You have direct and complete control over your data: it lives on your device, and you can view, modify, export (via system backup), or permanently delete it at any time.

We do not engage in any form of automated decision-making or user profiling.

16. Cross-Border Data Transfers

The App does not transmit any data, so no data export or cross-border transfer takes place.

Because the App neither holds nor transmits any user data, a data breach on our side is not possible. If your device is lost, stolen, or unlocked by someone else and the records in the App are viewed as a result, that situation is beyond our control and we cannot accept responsibility for it.
